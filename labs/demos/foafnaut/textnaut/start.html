Foafnaut

A web browser with its own built-in engine. Sites to try, then our own pages.

Marks, from our tests with the native engine: slow means readable, but it takes more than 5 seconds to open or to answer while its scripts run; not yet means it does not draw usefully today.

On the Hub, Foafnaut and Textnaut can not read other sites. A site with a Hub copy opens in place from a copy kept on the Hub (openly licensed pages; the licence and the authors show under the page). The others need the Foafnaut app or Textnaut in a terminal (npx @foafos/textnaut).
