Right to left and mixed direction

Each dir="rtl" line below starts at the right edge, and its words run from right to left. Latin words and numbers inside it keep their own left-to-right order (the Unicode Bidirectional Algorithm, UAX 9), as in a browser. Lean finds the levels, reorders each line and shapes each run in its direction; the Hebrew and Arabic letters come from fallback fonts.
