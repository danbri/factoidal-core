Writing systems and direction

One line per writing system. The engine holds two fonts: Noto Sans Regular (3,884 glyphs) and, for the characters that it lacks, Noto Sans Armenian Regular. Green rows draw from Noto Sans, blue rows from the fallback font. Red rows are in neither font and draw as empty boxes (.notdef). The counts are characters of the sample that a font maps, read from its cmap table.

Fallback fonts landed with issue 1274; only Armenian has a fallback font so far. Gaps: no bidirectional layout or right-to-left shaping (issue 1275).

Right to left and mixed direction

In a browser, each dir="rtl" line below starts at the right edge, and its words run from right to left. Latin words and numbers inside it keep their own left-to-right order (the Unicode Bidirectional Algorithm, UAX 9). Here the Hebrew and Arabic letters are boxes, so the order of the Latin words and numbers shows what the engine does.
