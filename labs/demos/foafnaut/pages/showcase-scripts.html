Right to left and mixed direction

In a browser, each dir="rtl" line below starts at the right edge, and its words run from right to left. Latin words and numbers inside it keep their own left-to-right order (the Unicode Bidirectional Algorithm, UAX 9). Here the Hebrew and Arabic letters are boxes, so the order of the Latin words and numbers shows what the engine does.
