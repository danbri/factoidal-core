Shadow DOM and sandboxed frames

The shadow trees, their slots and their style rules are computed by the built-in engine; the page is laid out on the flat tree. The frame's sandbox and its messages are decided by the built-in engine too.

A card component

Each card keeps its own markup and style in a shadow root. The page's own content goes into the card's slots.

A declarative card Its shadow root comes from the HTML parser (template shadowrootmode).
A card made by a scriptThe script calls attachShadow and adds the slots.

A sandboxed frame

The frame below has sandbox="allow-scripts": its scripts run in a session of their own, with a heap of their own and an opaque origin. It can not reach this page's document, and this page can not reach its document. The two talk only with postMessage.
