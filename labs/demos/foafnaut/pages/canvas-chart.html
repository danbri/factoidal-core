A Canvas 2D chart, drawn by the built-in engine

Bars, a line and text labels drawn with canvas.getContext('2d') and animated with requestAnimationFrame. The page's calls go to Lean once a frame as one display list; Lean parses the colours and the font, builds the paths, shapes the labels and rasterises every pixel.
