Foafnaut

A web browser with its own built-in engine. Sites to try, then our own pages.

Marks: slow means usable but slow to open or to answer while its scripts run; not yet means it does not draw usefully today.

  • Wikipedia, mobile skin: the same article in one column. Slow
  • Simple English Wikipedia: shorter articles in plain words. Slow
  • Wiktionary: a dictionary entry. Slow
