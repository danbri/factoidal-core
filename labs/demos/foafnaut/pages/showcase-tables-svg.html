Tables drawn as SVG

The tables of showcase-tables.html, drawn as SVG: a caption bar, a header row with a rule, zebra rows, numbers aligned right on their last digit, a cell that spans two columns, and a totals row. tools/showcase-pages.py computes the column positions; the labels are glyph outlines from Noto Sans.

This is the workaround. HTML table layout is not implemented (issue 1272), and SVG text is not drawn (issue 1270).
