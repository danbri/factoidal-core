What a colour glyph needs, drawn in SVG

A colour emoji glyph is layers of filled shapes, each with its own colour (the COLR and CPAL tables of OpenType). The engine already draws such layers in SVG. These three faces are hand-drawn SVG, not font glyphs.

Emoji draw from Noto Emoji, an outline font in one colour (SIL Open Font License), which the browser fetches only for a page with emoji (issue 1276). Each emoji is one glyph, and so is each sequence: the font's ligatures join ZWJ sequences, flags, the Scottish tag sequence and keycaps. A skin tone draws as the plain emoji, since the font has one form, and text and emoji presentation look alike.

Not yet: colour glyphs (the COLR and CPAL tables above).
