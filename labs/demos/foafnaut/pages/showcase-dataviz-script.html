Data visualisation with a script

A bar chart in the manner of D3: a script joins a data array to SVG rectangles, sizes each bar with setAttribute, adds one bar with createElementNS, and grows the bars with requestAnimationFrame. A click on the chart sorts the bars.

The script runs in the engine chosen on the settings page (QuickJS-NG unless you choose another). Each change it makes is a DOM command that Lean checks and applies (issue 1271). With scripts off, the bars below have their final static sizes.
