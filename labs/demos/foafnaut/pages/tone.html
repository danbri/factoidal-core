Tone

A 440 Hz sine wave through the Web Audio API: new AudioContext(), an OscillatorNode into destination, start() after a click. The engine renders the graph in Lean and writes it to the sound output. A page can start sound only after the person has used it (a click or a key), as in Chrome and Safari; the option --dangerously-permit-audio turns this rule off.
