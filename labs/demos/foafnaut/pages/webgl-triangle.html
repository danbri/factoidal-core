WebGL 1 and 2, checked by the browser

Two canvases, one with getContext('webgl2') and GLSL ES 3.00, one with getContext('webgl') and GLSL ES 1.00. Lean parses both dialects with one front end, translates each program to the same small WGSL subset as WebGPU pages use, checks it, clamps its indices and bounds-checks every buffer write and draw.
