Ray marching

A small scene of signed distance fields: a torus and two spheres over a chequered floor, lit by one light. Each pixel marches a ray through the scene with a fixed step count. The same picture is written twice, in WGSL for WebGPU and in GLSL ES 3.00 for WebGL 2.

API: WebGPU · WebGL 2

Quality: low (160 × 100, 40 steps, no shadow) · high (480 × 300, 64 steps, soft shadow)

Frame rate: 30 a second (default) · the display's rate (60, 120 or more) · as fast as it goes (a measurement: frame time is draw to done)

This page needs scripts and WebGPU or WebGL 2.

The script has not run.

To keep the load small: at most 64 steps a pixel, a low resolution unless asked, at most 30 frames a second unless asked (?fps=display draws on every animation frame of the display, up to its refresh rate; ?fps=max draws short bursts of frames, each waited for until the GPU is done, to measure how long a frame takes), movement by elapsed time (so a slow frame does not slow the scene), and no frames while the page is hidden or paused. In Foafnaut the built-in engine checks both shaders against its GPU subset (loops with literal bounds, no break, no user functions), which is why the distance function is written out in place.
