Ray marching

A small scene of signed distance fields: a torus and two spheres over a chequered floor, lit by one light. Each pixel marches a ray through the scene with a fixed step count. The same picture is written twice, in WGSL for WebGPU and in GLSL ES 3.00 for WebGL 2.

API: WebGPU · WebGL 2

Quality: low (160 × 100, 40 steps, no shadow) · high (480 × 300, 64 steps, soft shadow)

This page needs scripts and WebGPU or WebGL 2.

The script has not run.

To keep the load small: at most 64 steps a pixel, a low resolution unless asked, at most 30 frames a second, movement by elapsed time (so a slow frame does not slow the scene), and no frames while the page is hidden or paused. In Foafnaut the built-in engine checks both shaders against its GPU subset (loops with literal bounds, no break, no user functions), which is why the distance function is written out in place. Written for this project; MIT, like the rest of the repository.
