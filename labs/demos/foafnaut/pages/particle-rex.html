One-button game. Tap, click, or press space, Enter, W or the up arrow; hold the key to hold. In a zone where a tap turns, the arrow keys and W, A, S and D turn toward that direction and do nothing when you already go that way. Down and S do nothing in the jump zones. Shimmering rainbow things are good to collect. Bombs with a red glow cost a heart. Each zone shows what a tap does. Press M to mute. In god mode, press G for the cheat panel.
