Stress tests

Heavy pages. Each one asks a lot of the engine or the GPU, so it may take seconds to open, scroll slowly or keep the processor busy. They are here to be measured and made faster; the light pages are in the demo index.

A long animated page: about 2,300 primitives a frame with strokes, gradients, a web font and SVG, redrawn by a script. The measured page of core issue 450 (spinal_perf.ts, spinal_bisect.ts, the real-page survey).

Shader gallery: two triangles

Full-canvas fragment shaders up to 800 × 600 (1600 × 1000 if asked), with 96- and 256-step loops, every frame.

WebGPU compute: a force-directed graph of 1,000 nodes

A WebGPU compute pass over 1,000 nodes each frame, then a draw of every node and edge.

FOAF explorer, verified overlap check (WebAssembly)

A script-driven graph layout with a WebAssembly check of every frame.
