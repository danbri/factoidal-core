The page of tests/audio/page_tone.sh (issue https://github.com/danbri/factoidal-core/issues/1169). A key press starts a real-time AudioContext: a 440 Hz sine OscillatorNode into the destination, from 0 s to 1 s; at its ended event the page closes the context. At start the page renders the same graph with an OfflineAudioContext and logs a hash of its frames.
