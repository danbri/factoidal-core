## Findings so far
- Drift is W3C editing the served documents after 2007 (http->https in
  link/base attributes), not redirects. The 2007 REC zip
  https://www.w3.org/TR/2007/REC-grddl-tests-20070911/grddl-tests.zip
  (frozen TR space) has the original LOCAL inputs. 9 vendored local inputs
  differ from it: baseURI.html titleauthor.html xinclude1.html
  xhtmlWith*.html (6). Networked inputs are not in the zip; Wayback
  (web.archive.org) is not reachable through the proxy; cvsweb.w3.org is
  Team-only.
- GRDDL §3/§5: namespace/profile transformations come from a GRDDL RESULT of
  the namespace/profile document (triple <NS> grddl:namespaceTransformation TX,
  <P> grddl:profileTransformation TX), so discovery is recursive. Current
  library scans elements by local name instead (one level).
- grddl-tests "Tests with Multiple GRDDL Results": g:alternative groups need
  "at least one" of the results; g:subsumes marks the maximal result.
