# VP8 unfiltered YUV reconstruction

The raster reconstruction uses the ten keyframe luma subblock modes and the four whole-block prediction modes. Reconstructed neighbours are read from the padded YUV planes. Chroma buffers have half the luma stride and height. At the left and top frame edges, VP8's virtual predictor neighbours are supplied, and the rightmost luma subblocks obtain their above-right extension from the macroblock's top edge.

Signed spatial residual is added before byte saturation. Lean's `clipSample_toNat` states the resulting pixel value exactly. The differential crops the padded planes to the original image dimensions and compares them byte-for-byte with libwebp decoding with its loop filter disabled.

The loop filter, RGB conversion, alpha and browser integration follow this stage.
